Today, we’re announcing that Replit has acquired Atta, bringing its approach to business analysis and high-quality, purpose-built charting into Replit. Our mission is to make useful intelligence accessible to everyone. For businesses, that means giving the people closest to a problem the ability to investigate it, explain what they find, and act on their expertise.
